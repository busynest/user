pwa-auth

Five elements share one session: user-icon, user-drawer, user-login, user-settings, and email-reset. Listen for pwa- events, call the small API, restyle with --pwa_ variables.

npm GitHub introduction quick start

Drawer Button

Signed out, this says Subscribe and opens the drawer. Sign up there with any email. After you sign in, the button shows your photo or your initials, and the drawer shows your name, Settings, and the two markers slotted - before and slotted - default.

Login Page

The same forms, in the page. Use create a new account. The password needs at least 8 characters. A wrong password is written under the field. You can sign in before you open the verification email.

email-reset

Sends a reset link for the address you type. The same sentence appears whether or not an account uses that email.

Settings Page

Change your name, photo, email, or password, or delete the account. The name and photo are still there the next time you sign in. Settings in the drawer lands here.
